## Overview

Parameter extraction is a fundamental step in compact MOSFET modelling, directly affecting the accuracy and reliability of device simulation. Existing parameter extraction methods primarily focus on optimization algorithms and fitting strategies, while the influence of parameter sensitivity on the extraction process has received relatively limited attention. This paper proposes a sensitivity-guided parameter extraction strategy for compact MOSFET models under multi-bias conditions. A normalized sensitivity analysis is performed to evaluate the influence of individual model parameters on the drain current response over multiple operating conditions. Based on the sensitivity evaluation, parameter priorities are determined to guide the extraction process, aiming to improve the efficiency and robustness of parameter estimation. The proposed strategy is implemented on a Level-1 compact MOSFET model using simulated multi-bias current–voltage characteristics. Its performance is evaluated by comparing the proposed approach with a conventional extraction procedure in terms of fitting accuracy, convergence behaviour, and computational efficiency. The proposed method provides a practical and systematic strategy for compact MOSFET parameter extraction and demonstrates the effectiveness of incorporating parameter sensitivity into the extraction workflow.
