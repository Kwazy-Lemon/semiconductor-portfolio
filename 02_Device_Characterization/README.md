# Device Characterization

Methods and techniques for analyzing the electrical behavior of semiconductor devices through current–voltage (I–V) and capacitance–voltage (C–V) characterization.

---

## Overview

This section documents my study of semiconductor device characterization techniques. It focuses on understanding how electrical measurements are used to evaluate device performance, analyze device behavior, and extract important physical parameters.

The topics covered in this section establish the experimental and analytical foundation for MOSFET parameter extraction, compact modeling, and semiconductor device research.

---

## Objectives

The goals of this section are to:

- Understand the electrical behavior of semiconductor devices.
- Learn commonly used characterization techniques.
- Connect measurement results with physical device parameters.
- Build the foundation for MOSFET parameter extraction and compact modeling.
